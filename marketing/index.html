Straight answers

What does the free audit include?

A written, plain-English report: where customers find you, where they don't, how you compare on reviews, and the fixes that would win the most work. Every claim backed by something we measured. Yours to keep either way.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel with an email. We re-earn your business every 30 days — that's how you keep a vendor honest.

Which bundle is right for me?

If you're mostly losing leads — calls you're missing, inquiries that don't hear back — start with Starter. If you're established but need to show up better on Google and fill slow weeks, Growth is the move. Complete and Master are for operators who want the full system running. We'll talk it through on the call either way.

What about setup fees and ad spend?

Setup fees ($1,000–$2,500 depending on the scope), Google Ads spend, and SMS/phone overages aren't listed on the site — they vary by business and we walk through them in full on the discovery call. No surprises.

How long before I see results?

Missed-call text-back and follow-up sequences typically show results within the first week. Review counts start improving within 30–60 days. Google Maps rankings take longer — most clients see meaningful movement within 60–90 days, though competitive markets can take up to 6 months. We show you ranking screenshots every month so you can track progress.

I'm not tech savvy. How much do I have to do?

Almost nothing. You run your crew — we handle the website, Google, reviews, and call capture. The only skill required is reading a text and a short monthly report in plain English.

How is payment handled?

We send an invoice after your kickoff call. No credit card required upfront, no surprise charges. Everything confirmed before it goes out.
